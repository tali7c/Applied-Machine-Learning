\section*{Objective}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

  \item Build a diagnostic classifier where a false negative is the dangerous error.

  \item Use cross-validation properly on a small dataset.

  \item Reduce dimensionality and measure what it costs.

\end{itemize}

\section*{Datasets used in this assignment}

\textbf{B3 (Breast Cancer Wisconsin Diagnostic).}\\ Full descriptions, sources and loading snippets for all anchor datasets are in \texttt{Anchor-Datasets.pdf}, alongside this brief.

\section*{Instructions}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

  \item Use a train/test split (80/20 unless stated) and set \texttt{random\_state} for reproducibility.

  \item Fit every transformer inside a \texttt{Pipeline} so nothing is fitted on the test set.

  \item Report the metric named in the question. A number without units or context earns no marks.

  \item Every question asks for a short written observation. Code without commentary caps you at half marks.

  \item The lab quiz that follows will ask you to read, trace and modify \emph{your own} submitted code.

\end{itemize}

\textbf{Submit:}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]

  \item Notebook: \texttt{AML\_LA09\_<SAPID>.ipynb} with all cells executed and outputs visible.

  \item Report: 2--3 pages PDF, or a \texttt{README.md}, carrying your results table and observations.

  \item Do not upload datasets. Cite the source and include the loading code.

\end{itemize}

\section*{Section A (Easy) --- attempt any 2 of 3 \quad (2 $\times$ 2 = 4)}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{A\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]

  \item \textbf{Load and inspect} \QMarks{2}\\
    \emph{Dataset: B3.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*]
      \item Load the data, report the shape and the malignant/benign split.
      \item Explain in two lines where the 30 features come from -- they are not raw pixels.
      \item Report the three features most correlated with the diagnosis.
    \end{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{First classifier} \QMarks{2}\\
    \emph{Dataset: B3.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*]
      \item Fit logistic regression in a scaling pipeline with a stratified 80/20 split.
      \item Report accuracy, precision, recall and F1 on the malignant class.
      \item Report the number of malignant cases the model missed.
    \end{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{Cross-validation} \QMarks{2}\\
    \emph{Dataset: B3.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*]
      \item Run 10-fold stratified cross-validation and report mean and standard deviation of accuracy.
      \item Compare with your single-split figure from A2.
      \item State in two lines why the single split is unreliable on 569 rows.
    \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\section*{Section B (Medium) --- attempt any 1 of 2 \quad (1 $\times$ 4 = 4)}

\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{B\arabic*.}, leftmargin=*]

  \item \textbf{Optimising for recall} \QMarks{4}\\
    \emph{Dataset: B3.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*]
      \item Train logistic regression, SVM and random forest, and report recall on the malignant class for each.
      \item For your best model, lower the decision threshold until malignant recall reaches at least 0.99, and report the resulting precision and the number of false alarms.
      \item In 6--8 lines, argue what recall a screening tool should target and what the false alarms cost.
    \end{enumerate}

  \item \textbf{PCA and the cost of compression} \QMarks{4}\\
    \emph{Dataset: B3.}
    \begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), leftmargin=*]
      \item Apply PCA inside the pipeline retaining 2, 5, 10 and 20 components.
      \item Report cross-validated accuracy for each, plus the variance explained.
      \item Plot the first two components coloured by diagnosis and explain in 4--6 lines what PCA bought and what it cost.
    \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}
