\begin{center}{\LARGE\textbf{Anchor Datasets}}\\[0.25em]{\large \CourseCode}\\[0.25em]\normalsize Reference for lab assignments LA1--LA10\end{center}\vspace{0.4em}\hrule\vspace{0.8em}

The ten lab assignments reuse three families of dataset. The point is that you learn the methods rather than spending every fortnight wrestling a new file format. Download each one \emph{once} into a local \texttt{data/} folder and keep it there for the semester.

\subsection*{Anchor A -- Tabular records}
Mixed numeric and categorical rows, one record per entity. Used for EDA, regression, classification, scoring and anomaly work.\\[0.3em]
\begin{center}\footnotesize\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.7cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{6.4cm}@{}}\toprule\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Dataset} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{Notes}\\
\midrule
A1 & California Housing & \texttt{\footnotesize sklearn.datasets.fetch\_california\_housing} & 20{,}640 rows, 8 numeric features, numeric target MedHouseVal (\$100k units). Ships with scikit-learn; no download.\\
A2 & Telco Customer Churn & \texttt{\footnotesize Kaggle: blastchar/telco-customer-churn} & 7{,}043 rows, 21 mixed features, binary target Churn. Download the CSV once and keep it in \texttt{data/}.\\
A3 & Statlog German Credit & \texttt{\footnotesize OpenML: credit-g (id 31)} & 1{,}000 rows, 20 mixed features, binary target class (good/bad credit). Fetch with \texttt{sklearn.datasets.fetch\_openml('credit-g', version=1)}.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
\subsection*{Anchor B -- Images and image-derived features}
Pixel grids and features measured from images. Used for image classification, clustering on images and diagnostic tasks.\\[0.3em]
\begin{center}\footnotesize\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.7cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{6.4cm}@{}}\toprule\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Dataset} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{Notes}\\
\midrule
B1 & scikit-learn digits & \texttt{\footnotesize sklearn.datasets.load\_digits} & 1{,}797 images of 8$\times$8 greyscale handwritten digits, 10 classes. A small stand-in for MNIST that trains in seconds.\\
B2 & MNIST (full) & \texttt{\footnotesize sklearn.datasets.fetch\_openml('mnist\_784')} & 70{,}000 images of 28$\times$28 greyscale digits. Use a subsample of 10{,}000 unless you have time to spare.\\
B3 & Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic) & \texttt{\footnotesize sklearn.datasets.load\_breast\_cancer} & 569 rows, 30 features computed from digitised images of fine-needle aspirates, binary target malignant/benign.\\
B4 & PlantVillage (leaf subset) & \texttt{\footnotesize Kaggle: emmarex/plantdisease} & Leaf photographs labelled by crop and disease. Use the tomato subset (10 classes) resized to 64$\times$64 to keep runtimes sane.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
\subsection*{Anchor C -- Sequences and text}
Data with an order that matters, or free text. Used for time-series regression, text classification and signal work.\\[0.3em]
\begin{center}\footnotesize\begin{tabular}{@{}p{0.7cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{3.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.1cm}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{6.4cm}@{}}\toprule\textbf{ID} & \textbf{Dataset} & \textbf{Source} & \textbf{Notes}\\
\midrule
C1 & Daily stock OHLCV & \texttt{\footnotesize Kaggle: any single-ticker daily history, or yfinance} & One row per trading day with Open/High/Low/Close/Volume. Pick one ticker and at least three years of history.\\
C2 & SMS Spam Collection & \texttt{\footnotesize UCI ML Repository: SMS Spam Collection} & 5{,}574 English SMS messages labelled ham/spam. A single tab-separated file.\\
C3 & UCI Human Activity Recognition & \texttt{\footnotesize UCI: Human Activity Recognition Using Smartphones} & 561 features engineered from accelerometer and gyroscope windows, 6 activity classes, subject-wise train/test split supplied.\\
\bottomrule\end{tabular}\end{center}
